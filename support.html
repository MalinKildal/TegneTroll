TegneTroll Support

TegneTroll is a simple drawing app for kids — pick a color, pick a thickness, and draw. Drawings save locally on your device, and you can export a picture to your phone's photo gallery.

Common questions

  • How do I save a drawing? Tap "Lagre" at the top of the drawing screen.
  • How do I get a drawing into my photo gallery? Tap "Del til galleri" — you'll be asked for permission the first time.
  • I made a mistake — how do I undo it? Use the undo arrow next to the thickness buttons, or the trash icon to clear the whole drawing.
  • Can I continue an old drawing? Yes — tap it in the gallery to reopen and keep drawing.
