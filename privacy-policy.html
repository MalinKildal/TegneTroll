Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2, 2026

TegneTroll ("we", "our", or "the app") is a simple drawing app for kids. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information.

Information We Collect

None. TegneTroll does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. There are no user accounts, no analytics, and no advertising. The app does not require an internet connection to draw or save your work.

Your Drawings

Drawings you create are saved only in the app's private storage on your own device. We never see them, and they are never uploaded anywhere.

Photo Library Access

If you tap "Del til galleri" (Share to gallery), TegneTroll asks for permission to add a picture to your device's Photos app. The app only ever adds pictures you choose to export — it never reads, browses, or otherwise accesses your existing photos.

Third-Party Services

TegneTroll does not use any third-party services, analytics, or advertising SDKs.

Children's Privacy

TegneTroll is designed for children. Because the app collects no personal information whatsoever, no data about children (or anyone else) is gathered, stored, or shared.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy ever changes, the "Last updated" date above will be updated accordingly.
